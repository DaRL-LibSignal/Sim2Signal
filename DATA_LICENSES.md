- **Hangzhou** (`hangzhou_1x1_*/`, `hangzhou_4x4_gudang_18041610_1h/`): from [traffic-signal-control.github.io](https://traffic-signal-control.github.io/) (Wei et al., CoLight, CIKM 2019), redistributed with the authors' permission.

Other folders in `data/raw_data/` come from LibSignal, are not part of the benchmark, and are not covered here. If you use a network, please cite Sim2Signal and the original source.
